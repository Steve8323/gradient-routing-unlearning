Random-interconnected gradient routing — does scatter-then-prune beat a clean region?

Idea (yours): tag a random, interconnected subset of weights as "waste", route the forget capability into them, prune them at test time. Testbed: MNIST MLP 784-256-256-10 (h=128 for the agent runs), forget={0..4} vs retain={5..9}, CPU, multi-seed, independently replicated. Chance ≈ 0.20.

3 of 4 favor scattered routing. Random wins wherever the benefit is keeping every neuron usable (deep deletion, soft absorption, packing many capabilities into shared neurons). Structural wins only on relearning-resistance, because cutting whole pathways leaves the attacker fewer channels.

Naive scattered routing deletes forget (0.0) but the interconnection collapses retain (0.20). Ablation-aware training — train retain on the already-pruned net so main can't lean on waste — fixes it: forget 0.0, retain 0.95 with random weights. Control proves the deletion is real (same prune on a normal net leaves forget at 0.93).

Mechanistic punchline: absorption (mislabeled forget still deleted) and co-adaptation collateral (retain collapses on prune) are the same knob — does retain's training see the waste region? See-it → absorption + collateral; don't-see-it → clean + no absorption. The frontier (panel C) shows you can dial a middle setting for partial absorption at near-full retain, and random gives a strictly better frontier than structural.

Bonus (bigger than random-vs-structural)

Routing-then-prune — either partition — is dramatically more tamper-resistant than ordinary unlearning (panel F). Gradient-ascent unlearning snaps back to 0.91 forget accuracy in 10 attack steps; routed+pruned needs ~30 just to half-recover. Prune-localized deletion resists relearning in a way suppression (GA/NPO/RMU) does not.

Caveats

Tiny model, MNIST, a class-split as a "capability" proxy. Effects are existence-proof dynamics on a toy, multi-seed and replicated but not a frontier claim. Absorption in the naive condition is partly entangled with the collateral; cleanest absorption readout is the frontier (panel C) where retain is held fixed. Magnitudes are honest means; tamper and multicap gaps are consistent in sign across seeds but modest at saturation.
